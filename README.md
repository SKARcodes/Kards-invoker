
**Kards Invoker** is a card game developed in **Unity** with **C#**.

Players face off in 1v1 duels where they summon powerful creatures (summons)
and use buff and debuff cards to win the battle.


# Main Mechanics
- **Summons**: Creatures with attack, defense, and health
- **Buffs**: Cards that improve your summons
- **Debuffs**: Cards that weaken the enemy
- **Mana System**: Limited resources for playing cards
- **Battle Phases**: Structured turns with preparation and combat

# How to Play

1. **Summon Selection**: Choose which creature to summon
2. **Preparation Phase**: Draw cards and prepare your strategy
3. **Combat Phase**: 10 seconds for your summon to attack
4. **Win/Defeat**: Win when you destroy the enemy summon

# Technologies Used

- **Engine**: Unity 6000.4.6f1
- **Language**: C# 11+
- **Architecture**: Object-Oriented ScriptableObjects
- **Patterns**: Singleton (BattleManager), Strategy (CardEffect)
